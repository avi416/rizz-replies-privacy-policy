Privacy Policy

Rizz Replies · Last updated: October 8, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Rizz Replies ("we", "us", "our", or the "App") handles information when you use our mobile application. Rizz Replies is an AI-powered text reply assistant that helps you generate suggested replies to messages you have received.

By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy and to our Terms of Use. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

2. Information We Collect

Rizz Replies is designed to collect as little information as possible. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifier. From version 1.3.0 the App shows advertising in some countries, as described in section 5.

The App processes the following information:

  • Message text you provide: when you paste a received message into the App and request a suggested reply, that text is sent to our backend service for AI processing.
  • Screenshots you choose to add: if you add a screenshot of a conversation, it is sent for the sole purpose of reading the messages out of it. See section 4.
  • Reply preferences: the tone, goal, length, relationship and reply language you choose for a request.
  • Writing-style examples you choose to provide: processed on your device to build a style profile. The examples themselves are not sent. See section 7.
  • A random install ID: on first launch the App creates a random identifier that is not derived from your device, your advertising ID or you. It is sent with each request so our backend can count how many screenshots one installation has read today. See section 5.
  • Your approximate country, per request: our backend reads the country our network provider (Cloudflare) attaches to each request, only to decide whether ads may be shown to you. It is not stored, logged or linked to you. See section 5.
  • Data collected by the advertising SDK, where ads are shown: see section 5.
  • Your Rizz Pro purchase, if you subscribe: the purchase token Google Play gives the App, used to confirm the subscription with Google Play. See section 6.

We do not collect:

  • Login or account information (the App has no login system).
  • Precise location data.
  • Contacts.
  • Camera or microphone access.
  • Photo library access — the App uses the Android photo picker, which hands over only the single image you select. The App holds no storage permission.

We do not use an analytics SDK. The advertising SDK described in section 5 collects its own data, including advertising and device identifiers, where ads are shown.

3. How We Use Information

What you submit is used solely to:

  • Generate AI-suggested replies and return them to your device.
  • Apply basic safety and moderation checks required to operate the service responsibly.

We do not use your text to build user profiles, to advertise to you, or for any purpose unrelated to generating your requested reply. Nothing you write, paste or capture — no message, reply, screenshot or style description — is ever passed to the advertising SDK, in any form.

4. AI Processing

To generate replies, the App sends what you submit to our backend at https://rizz-replies.onrender.com, which forwards the request to the Google Gemini API (provided by Google) for AI processing. The generated reply suggestions are then returned to your device.

About screenshots: a screenshot is resized and re-encoded on your device before it leaves, and is sent for the single purpose of reading the messages out of it. It is not stored — not by the App, and not by our backend. What comes back is the extracted text, which from that point behaves exactly like text you pasted yourself; the image is discarded once the response returns.

By using the App, you understand that:

  • What you submit is transmitted over the internet to our backend and to Google's Gemini API for the sole purpose of generating a reply.
  • Google's handling of data sent to the Gemini API is governed by Google's own terms and privacy policies.
  • You should avoid submitting sensitive personal information (such as passwords, financial details, government IDs, or health information), and avoid screenshots containing anything you would not paste as text.

5. Advertising

From version 1.3.0 the App shows ads to keep it free: a banner, occasional full-screen ads at natural breaks (for example when you start a new message), and an optional short video you can choose to watch to unlock an extra screenshot read on a day you have used up your free ones. Pasting text is always free and is never limited. Rizz Pro subscribers see no ads at all (see section 6).

Where ads are not shown. The App shows no ads at all to users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, or where the country cannot be determined. There, the advertising SDK is not started, nothing is sent to it, and screenshot reads are not limited. To decide this, our backend reads the country attached to each request by our network provider (Cloudflare). The country is not stored, logged or linked to you.

Who provides the ads. Ads are served through Unity LevelPlay, Unity's ad mediation platform (formerly ironSource), which sources ads from advertisers through the ironSource ad exchange and the Unity Ads ad network. Where ads are shown, their SDKs collect, according to their own disclosures: approximate location, device and advertising identifiers, interactions with the ads, app diagnostics, and, for Unity Ads, purchase history, for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention and security. Their handling of that data is governed by their own policies:

  • ironSource (LevelPlay): ironSource Mobile Privacy Policy
  • Unity Ads: Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy

Your choices.

  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in your device's Settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Inside a Unity ad you can change your privacy choices by tapping its Data Privacy icon.

The install ID. The random install ID described in section 2 is used only to count how many screenshots one installation has read today. Our backend never logs it and never stores it as-is: it keeps only a one-way hash made with a random key that is replaced every day, held in memory and discarded daily. It is not shared with the advertising SDK. Clearing the App's data creates a new one.

6. Rizz Pro (optional subscription)

From version 1.4.0 you can subscribe to Rizz Pro, a monthly subscription sold through Google Play. It removes all ads — the advertising SDK is not started on your device while you are a subscriber — and raises your daily screenshot reads to the number shown when you subscribe. It renews automatically every month until you cancel. Pasting text and generating replies stay free for everyone.

Payment. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play, under Google's terms and privacy policy. We never see your card or any other payment details.

What we receive, and why. When you subscribe, each time the App starts, and when you tap “Restore purchase”, the App sends the purchase token Google Play gave it to our backend, which asks Google Play (through the Google Play Developer API) whether that subscription is active. The App also sends the token with each screenshot you add, so our backend can apply the subscriber limit. The token and Google's answer are used only to provide Rizz Pro.

What we keep. Our backend does not store the purchase token. In memory only, it keeps a one-way hash of the token with Google's answer for at most one hour, and a count of today's screenshot reads under a hash made with a random key that is replaced every day. Nothing is written to disk, and a restart erases it. The token is never logged, never counted in our statistics, and never shared with the advertising SDK or anyone else. On your device, the App stores the token and the date until which the subscription was last confirmed.

Managing or cancelling. You can manage or cancel the subscription at any time in Google Play (Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions), or from the App's Settings, which opens that page.

7. Local Storage

The following are stored locally on your device using the device's standard preferences storage:

  • Saved replies and your reply history.
  • Your settings, including interface language and reply language.
  • Your writing-style profile, if you create one.
  • The random install ID, the last advertising settings received from our backend, and when an ad was last shown (timestamps and counts only).
  • If you subscribe to Rizz Pro: the purchase token and the date until which the subscription was last confirmed.

This data stays on your device, is not uploaded to our servers as history or as saved replies, and is removed if you uninstall the App or clear the App's data.

About the style profile: if you teach the App to write more like you, the profile is derived on your device from the examples you provide, and it is stored only on your device. Your example messages themselves are never uploaded. When you generate a reply with the profile switched on, a compact description of your style — such as typical length, formality and emoji use, not your messages — is sent with that request in the same way as your tone settings, and is not retained by our backend or stored as a profile by the AI provider. You can delete the profile at any time from Settings.

8. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We share what you submit only with:

  • Our backend service (https://rizz-replies.onrender.com), which acts as the intermediary that calls the AI provider.
  • Google Gemini API, strictly for the purpose of generating a reply you requested.
  • Google Play, only if you subscribe to Rizz Pro: the purchase token, to confirm the subscription is active.

Where ads are shown (see section 5), the advertising SDK collects its own data directly from your device, as its providers describe. We do not share your text, screenshots, or style description with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third parties, and nothing you submit is passed to the advertising SDK.

9. Data Retention

  • On your device: saved replies, history and your style profile remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App.
  • On our backend: we do not permanently store the messages you submit. Your text is processed in transit to generate a reply and is not retained as a permanent record by us. Screenshots are held only for the duration of the request and are never written to disk. A style description sent with a request is treated like the request text: processed, not retained. Temporary, short-lived processing data (such as request logs needed to operate the service) may exist briefly for reliability and abuse-prevention purposes and is not used to identify you.
  • AI provider: Google's retention practices for data sent to the Gemini API are governed by Google.
  • Rizz Pro: the purchase token is not stored by our backend; a hash of it is held in memory for at most one hour, and a daily read count until the end of the day (see section 6).

10. Children's Privacy

Rizz Replies is not intended for children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and its ads are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has used the App, please contact us so we can address the matter.

11. Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect data in transit, including HTTPS encryption between the App, our backend, and the AI provider. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please use the App responsibly and avoid submitting sensitive personal information.

12. User Controls

  • Clear history: you can clear your reply history at any time from the App's Settings.
  • Clear saved replies: you can delete saved replies at any time from the App's Settings.
  • Delete your writing-style profile: you can remove it at any time from the App's Settings.
  • Remove a screenshot: you can take a screenshot back out before generating anything from it.
  • Advertising: reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalisation in your device settings; see section 5.
  • Uninstall: uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data associated with it, including the install ID.
  • Choose what to submit: you decide what text and which screenshots to give the App. Avoid submitting information you do not want to be processed by an AI service.

13. Languages

The App's interface is available in 20 languages, and replies can be generated in the same 20. Your language choices are stored on your device and change nothing about what is collected or shared: the same text goes to the same provider regardless of which language you read or write in.
